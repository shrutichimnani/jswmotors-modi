What is JSW Motors?
JSW Motors is the JSW Group’s passenger vehicle company, established in 2024 to build next-generation, sustainable vehicles for India. Its greenfield manufacturing facility is in Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar, Maharashtra.
Who is Gautam Modi Group?
Gautam Modi Group is an automotive group whose family roots in the business go back to 1932. It represents brands including Hyundai, Mahindra, Audi, Kia and MG, and now brings JSW Motors to Thane and Kalyan. Read our story.
When will JSW Motors vehicles be available?
Launch details haven’t been announced yet. Register your interest and we’ll let you know as soon as there’s news.
Where can I see JSW Motors vehicles?
At our showrooms in Thane (Times Square, LBS Marg) and Kalyan (Atlantic, Kalyan–Bhiwandi Road).
How do I book a test drive?
Test drives will open once vehicles are available. Register your interest or contact us, and our team will reach out when bookings open.
Where will my JSW Motors car be serviced?
At our JSW Motors Modi Thane workshop in Kothari Compound, Manpada, Thane West.
What happens after I register?
Our team will keep you updated about the JSW Motors launch by phone, SMS, WhatsApp or email. You can withdraw your consent at any time. See our Privacy Policy.
