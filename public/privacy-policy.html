This Privacy Policy explains how Gautam Modi Group (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit this website and register your interest in JSW Motors and its upcoming vehicles.

1. Information we collect

When you use the “Register Interest” form, we collect:

  • Your full name

  • Your city
  • Your consent to receive updates

We may also receive basic technical information, such as your browser type and pages visited, when you use the site.

2. How we use your information

  • To keep you updated about the launch of JSW Motors and its upcoming vehicles.
  • To contact you by phone, SMS, WhatsApp or email about launch updates, reservations and related offers, where you have agreed to this.

We process your information only with your consent, which you give by ticking the consent box on the form.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with JSW Motors and with service providers who help us run this website and contact you, only as needed for the purposes above and under obligations to keep it confidential. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as needed to keep you updated about the launch and to meet our legal obligations, after which we delete it or make it anonymous.

5. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your rights

Under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may:

  • Ask to see the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or update it.
  • Withdraw your consent or ask us to delete your information at any time.
  • Make a complaint about how we handle your information.

To use any of these rights, or to stop receiving updates, contact us using the details below.

7. Cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics or similar tools in future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.
